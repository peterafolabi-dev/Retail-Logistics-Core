# Returns and Refunds

Items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused, unworn, and in original packaging with all tags attached.

To start a return, contact support with your order number and reason for return.

Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout.

Some items — such as personal care products, opened electronics, and made-to-order items — are not eligible for return.

Damaged or incorrect items received should be reported within 48 hours of delivery for a replacement or full refund.
