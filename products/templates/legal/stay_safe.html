Stay safe from scams

Scammers sometimes pretend to be online stores. Here's how to tell a real RedCart message from a fake one.

RedCart will never ask for your card PIN, OTP, BVN or password — not by phone, SMS, WhatsApp, email or social media. Anyone who asks is a scammer. Hang up or don't reply.

Golden rules

  • Only pay on the RedCart checkout page. Payments go through Paystack on our website. We never ask you to transfer money to a personal bank account.
  • Prepaid orders are fully paid. A rider must not ask you for extra cash, a "delivery fee" or a "customs fee" on a prepaid order.
  • Cash on Delivery: pay only the exact amount shown in your order on the RedCart website.
  • Check the website address. Before signing in or paying, make sure you're on our real site and the address bar shows a padlock.
  • We don't send "you've won" prizes. Real promotions appear on our website. You never pay a fee to collect a prize.

Common scams to watch for

Fake delivery agentA caller says your parcel is held and asks for a "release fee" or your OTP. Your order status is always on your account page.
Fake WhatsApp "support"Someone messages you on WhatsApp using our name or logo and asks for payment or codes. RedCart never takes payments or handles refunds over WhatsApp{% if whatsapp_number_display %}, and our only number is {{ whatsapp_number_display }}{% endif %}.
Too-good-to-be-true dealsAds or social posts with huge discounts that send you to another website or a bank transfer. Real RedCart deals are on our site.
Refund or overpayment callsA caller says you were overpaid and asks you to send money back. We only refund through the original payment method or your RedCart wallet.
Phishing linksEmails or texts with a link to "verify your account". Sign in by typing our address yourself instead of clicking the link.

Think you've been targeted?

  1. Don't send money or share any codes.
  2. If you shared card details, call your bank right away to block the card.
  3. Report it to us through the Contact us page with screenshots, the phone number or link used, and your order number if you have one.
